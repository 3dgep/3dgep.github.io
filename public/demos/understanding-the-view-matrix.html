up = (0, 1, 0). Move the target (the orange cross) with WASD, up/down with QE, faster with Shift (click the view first): the eye stays put and the camera turns to follow. Drag in the camera view to move the eye around the target; use the wheel to move closer.

V = (T(eye) Ry(yaw) Rx(pitch))⁻¹

Drag in the camera view to look around. Move with WASD, up/down with QE, faster with Shift (click the view first). Pitch is limited to ±89° so the camera can't flip over.

V = (T₁ R T₀)⁻¹ = T₀⁻¹ R⁻¹ T₁⁻¹

Drag in the camera view to rotate the arcball: the drag is mapped onto a sphere and turned into the rotation quaternion R. T₀ moves the camera back along its z axis, T₁ moves it to the object.

View matrix V

Camera matrix M = V⁻¹

The rows of the 3×3 part of V are the camera's right, up and backward axes (the camera looks down −z); in M they are the columns, and the last column of M is the eye position.
